The card's record

Fresh scoreboard -- the pick-first card started Aug 19, 2026 and grades itself from day one. Every shown pick lands here as a WIN or LOSS at $1 a slip, settled by Kalshi itself. Give it a few weeks before trusting it with real beer money.
How this card works, in one breath: the sharpest sportsbooks in the world publish their opinion as prices; we strip out their commission to get an honest probability, then check what Kalshi's crowd charges for the same thing. When the crowd sells the sharps' pick at least 4¢ under the sharps' own number (after Kalshi's fee), it makes the card. We never pick against the sharps, never chase a "cheap" longshot, and anything over +15¢ is treated as bad data and suppressed. Props (first-5-innings, totals, strikeouts) are the main event -- moneylines tag along. Sharps must lean at least 55% -- coin flips don't get picks. Every market we evaluate is logged to sports_picks.csv, shown or not, and every shown pick is graded by Kalshi's own settlement in sports_results.csv. The record above is the only reason to trust (or ignore) this card.
