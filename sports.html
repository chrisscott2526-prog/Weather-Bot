How this card works, in one breath: the sharpest sportsbooks in the world publish their opinion as prices; we strip out their commission to get an honest probability, then check what Kalshi's crowd charges for the same thing. When the crowd sells the sharps' pick at least 4¢ under the sharps' own number (after Kalshi's fee), it makes the card. We never pick against the sharps, never chase a "cheap" longshot, and anything over +15¢ is treated as bad data and suppressed. Props (first-5-innings, totals, strikeouts) are the main event -- moneylines tag along. The card covers MLB, NFL, college football, the NBA (once its games are inside the scan window) and tour-level tennis; golf is deliberately absent because the odds feed carries only tournament-winner longshots for it -- no matchup lines means no honest pick, so none is invented. Every market we evaluate is logged to sports_picks.csv, shown or not, and every shown pick is graded by Kalshi's own settlement in sports_results.csv. The record above is the only reason to trust (or ignore) this card.
