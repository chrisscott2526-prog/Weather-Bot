How to read this board: the big number is the most recent National Weather Service reading at the exact station each Kalshi market settles on. The time under it is when the station took that reading — not when this page loaded — shown on your own clock. The thin bar drains over 60 minutes; a red card means the reading is over an hour old and you should not trust it with money. "High so far" is today's running maximum, computed on the spot from the same raw poll log as the big number — the swoop board computes its highs from the identical log with the identical rule, so the two can never disagree. "Yesterday" is different in kind: it is the official settled outcome — the temperature range Kalshi's own markets paid out on, straight from the exchange's result field, never our thermometer.

The money box on each card is the day-of lane. The bot buys a city only while that city's own clock reads 9:00–10:59 AM (each card shows that window on your clock), $1 a bet, one bet per city per day. When it bought, the box says which bracket, at what price, and why — what share of the ~80 forecast members voted for that bracket and how today's forecast compares with what settled yesterday. When it didn't buy, the box says exactly which rule said no (bracket too weak, price too high, price a long shot, no fresh forecast). A red box means the bot tried to buy and the order failed — usually an empty wallet — or that no run reached the city in its window. This page updates itself every 2 minutes and whenever you press Refresh, and it can never be fresher than the last poll. For anything bet-sized, the judge is the Daily Climate Report on weather.gov.
