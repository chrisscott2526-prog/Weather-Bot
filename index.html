How to read this board: the big number is the most recent National Weather Service reading at the exact station each Kalshi market settles on. The time under it is when the station took that reading — not when this page loaded. The thin bar drains over 60 minutes; a red card means the reading is over an hour old and you should not trust it with money. "High so far" is today's running maximum from the same station. This page updates itself every 2 minutes and whenever you press Refresh, and it can never be fresher than the last 15-minute poll. For anything bet-sized, the judge is the Daily Climate Report on weather.gov.
